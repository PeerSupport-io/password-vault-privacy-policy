# PeerSupport Vault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

PeerSupport Vault is a browser extension that stores your website login credentials in an
encrypted vault on your own device.

## What data the extension handles

- Website login credentials you choose to save (website address, username, password, optional
  notes and tags).
- A local log of actions inside the extension (unlocks, fills, copies, exports and changes).
- Your preferences (auto-lock timer, clipboard timer, enabled sites).

## Where the data is stored

Everything is stored inside your Chrome profile on your computer. Credentials and the activity
log are encrypted with a key derived from your master password. The extension has no servers, no
accounts and no cloud storage.

## What is sent to us or anyone else

Nothing. The extension makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, telemetry,
advertising or crash reporting. Its security policy technically forbids network requests.

## What the extension can see on websites

By default, nothing. On websites where you enable autofill, the extension reads login form fields
in order to fill them and, if you choose, to offer to save a new login. When you click Fill in the
extension, it accesses only the tab you are viewing. It never fills or submits anything on its own.

## Sharing

We do not share, sell or transmit any data. There is nothing to share: the data never leaves your
device unless you export a backup file yourself.

## Your control

You can view, edit and delete every saved login, export an encrypted backup, and delete the entire
vault at any time from the extension's settings. Uninstalling the extension removes its storage.

## Children

The extension is intended for use by healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.
